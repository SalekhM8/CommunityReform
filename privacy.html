Community Reform CIC

Privacy Notice

How we handle the information you give us when you enquire about childcare, book an appointment, or contact our team.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Community Reform CIC is a community interest company based at 294 Edward Road, Birmingham, B5 7PH. We are an Ofsted registered provider (registration number 2868905). For the information described in this notice, we are the data controller. You can contact us about your information at info@crcic.co.uk or on 07359 725 058.

What we collect

When you complete our childcare enquiry form, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address, home address and postcode
  • How many children you have and their ages
  • Your employment status
  • Which benefits or financial support you receive (you are never asked for account details or logins)
  • What kind of childcare help you need
  • The appointment date and time you choose
  • Anything you choose to tell us in the notes box

We do not ask for documents, bank details, or logins to benefit accounts, and you should never send those to us. If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we will also hold the messages you send us.

Why we need it

To assess whether you may be eligible for funded childcare, to help you find suitable childcare, to arrange and confirm your appointment, and to keep a record of the support we have given. We rely on your consent, which you give when you tick the consent box and submit the enquiry form, and on our legitimate interest in providing advice to families in our community.

Who else sees it

  • Cal.com provides our appointment booking system. It processes your name, email address, phone number and appointment details to send your confirmation and to send you a reminder the day before. Cal.com acts as our processor under a data processing agreement. Their servers are in the United States, and the transfer is covered by the UK Government's approved safeguards (the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).
  • Our staff, who only see your information in order to help with your enquiry.
  • Where you ask us to, we may share details with a childcare provider to help you find a place. We will only do this with your agreement.

We never sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and appointment records for 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. If you ask us to delete your information sooner, we will do so unless we are required by law to keep it.

Keeping it safe

Our website and enquiry form are served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Appointment information is stored in our booking system, and access is limited to staff who need it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do any of these, contact info@crcic.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top of it.
