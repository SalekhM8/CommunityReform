What happens next

  1. A confirmation email is on its way to you now – please check your spam folder if it doesn’t arrive within 15 minutes.
  2. Our team at Community Reform CIC has been notified at info@crcic.co.uk.
  3. You’ll automatically receive a reminder email the day before your appointment.
  4. If you need to change or cancel, use the links in your confirmation email, or call 07359 725 058.
